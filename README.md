### Hi, I'm César

Biomedical engineer and backend developer based in Valencia, Spain.

I work on the intersection of medical imaging, healthcare interoperability and compliance tooling. Day job is Java/Spring on hospital information systems at [Laberit](https://www.laberit.com/). On the side I build small, focused tools around DICOM, FHIR and the regulatory surface that comes with shipping software into clinical environments (MDR, GDPR, SBOMs).

### What I work on

Computer vision applied to medical imaging. My final-year thesis ([RiuNet, UPV](https://riunet.upv.es/handle/10251/226903)) trained a multi-instance-learning classifier on histopathology whole-slide images using CONCH foundation-model embeddings, and measured inter-hospital fairness gaps on the AI4SkIN skin-cancer dataset. F1 0.89 weighted, inter-hospital gap 0.19. Bias measurement on SaMD is a topic I care about a lot.

Backend in Java / Spring Boot. Two years writing production code against HIS/RIS/PACS-adjacent systems. I am comfortable with JPA, transactional design, message-driven workflows (RabbitMQ), and the kind of integration plumbing that healthcare software inevitably needs.
